# Mitra AI – Hyperlocal AI Assistant for Farmers

Mitra AI is an AI-powered assistant designed to help farmers access important agricultural information in a simple and centralized platform.

It provides crop price updates, weather forecasts, government scheme information, crop disease detection, and farming advisory.

The goal is to make agricultural technology accessible and useful for rural farmers.

# Problem Statement

Farmers in rural areas often lack access to reliable real-time agricultural information.

Many farmers rely on traditional methods such as:

- Calling local markets to know crop prices
- Observing the sky to predict weather
- Hearing about government schemes through village announcements
- Identifying crop diseases through visual inspection

Because of this information gap, farmers may make poor farming or selling decisions.

Mitra AI aims to bridge this gap by providing all necessary farming information in a single intelligent platform.

---

# Solution

Mitra AI integrates multiple agricultural services into one AI assistant.

Key Features

• Crop Price Finder – shows mandi prices  
• Weather Updates – localized weather forecasts  
• Government Scheme Finder – suggests schemes for farmers  
• Crop Disease Detection – identifies diseases from plant images  
• Farming Advisory – provides farming recommendations

---

# Research Insights

A small farmer interview study was conducted to understand how farmers currently obtain agricultural information.

Key findings:

- Farmers usually ask traders or people in the market about crop prices
- Weather prediction is often done by observing environmental signs
- Government scheme information spreads through word of mouth
- Farmers visually inspect plants to identify crop problems

These insights helped shape the design of Mitra AI.
